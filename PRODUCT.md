## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice). No build step; single folder deploys to GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages. Fonts from Google Fonts. Photos live in `assets/photos/` and are dropped in by the user.

## Users

Primary: families in and around Porur / west Chennai (Tamil-speaking, mobile-first, arriving from Google Maps, Instagram or a WhatsApp referral) who are planning a family function — birthday, naming ceremony, puberty function, engagement, housewarming, small reception — and need one vendor to handle decoration, photos/video and return gifts on a tight budget. They are usually on a phone, often at night, deciding whether to call/WhatsApp. Trust is the job: "will they turn up on time and will it look nice for the money."

Secondary: gift buyers (return gifts in bulk for a function; small handmade items and hair accessories as gifts or treats), and the owner themselves, who posts product updates to the Google listing.

## Product Purpose

The site is Aura Events & Craft's first website (they currently have only a Google Business Profile and an Instagram account). It exists to convert a Maps/Instagram/referral visit into a WhatsApp or phone enquiry with an event date attached, and to make the business feel established and warm enough that a family will book without visiting the shop first.

Success: the visitor understands within seconds that Aura does decoration + photo/video + return gifts + crafts together, at a budget-friendly price, and taps WhatsApp/call or sends an enquiry with a date.

## Positioning

One shop that does the decor, the photography/videography AND the return gifts and craft items for a family function, at budget-friendly prices, with a 5.0 rating across 16 Google reviews. Most Chennai event planners subcontract photos and gifts; Aura's reviews mention all three coming from the same place. It is also a craft shop, so it has a physical product side (handmade gifts, pearl hair clips) that a pure planner cannot show.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before dazzle: every wow moment must still make it obvious who they are, what they do and how to reach them.
2. One thumb, one tap: the phone experience is the real one; WhatsApp/call are never more than one tap away.
3. Show the three-in-one: decor, photo/video and gifts from one shop is the story; sections should reinforce it rather than list services generically.
4. Real words only: reviews, address, hours and attributes are real; anything unconfirmed is a labelled placeholder.
5. Budget-friendly is a feature, not an apology: say it plainly and warmly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The venue itself advertises wheelchair access, gender-neutral toilets and LGBTQ+ friendliness; the site should meet WCAG AA contrast, respect reduced motion, and keep tap targets large for one-handed phone use.
